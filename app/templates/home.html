What Bank Buddy does

Bank Buddy is not a general chatbot — it is a purpose-built financial assistant. Here is exactly what it can do for you:

💰

Create personalized budgets

Bank Buddy builds a monthly budget around your real income and expenses, with clear Rand categories and practical tips to help you stick to it.

📊

Track your expenses

Upload a bank statement (PDF, CSV or Excel) and Bank Buddy summarises where your money goes — by merchant, by category, and by month.

🧠

Suggest saving strategies

Get realistic, South African–specific saving ideas that fit your income — from small weekly habits to long-term planning.

🛟

Recommend emergency funds

Bank Buddy calculates a sensible emergency-fund target based on your monthly expenses and helps you build it step by step.

📚

Provide financial literacy lessons

Simple, jargon-free lessons on budgeting basics, debt management, savings, and tax — explained in the language you choose.

💬

Answer financial questions conversationally

Ask Bank Buddy anything about money in English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, or Afrikaans — and it will respond in the same language.

🧾

Generate spending summaries

Get a clear breakdown of what you spent, where, and how it compares to your budget or last month.

🎯

Set and track savings goals

Create savings goals in your dashboard — like an emergency fund, school fees, or a new laptop — and track your progress against them.

🔔

Send reminders for financial goals

Bank Buddy prompts you about your savings goals and nudges you when you are falling behind — with encouragement, not judgment.
